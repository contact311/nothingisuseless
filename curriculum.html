Curriculum

First programme to be announced early April 2026.


We are currently developing programmes along the following lines:

  • Expanded ways of teaching and learning, including peer-to-peer sharing and knowledge creation
  • Making visible and transferable the hidden curriculum of children with more privileged backgrounds, such as forms of social literacy, communication, and mental habits
  • Cultivating a social environment for underprivileged students, modelled after the social ecosystem available to privileged students
  • Programmes centring overlooked or marginalized forms of knowledge
